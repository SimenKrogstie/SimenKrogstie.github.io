About

My name is Simen, a final-year Master's student in Data Science with a specialization in Geoinformatics at NMBU. I like building solutions that combine machine learning, spatial analysis, and automation — I've worked on projects ranging from image analysis to spatial mobility indicators and GIS processes. In spring 2026, I went on exchange at Wageningen University, where I deepened my background in remote sensing and geoinformatics.

Alongside my studies, I've worked as a Teaching Assistant for MLA210 at NMBU, supporting other students in geospatial coursework, and I've taken on leadership as Head of Events for Samfunnet i Ås. I also bring a few years of customer-facing experience as a Banking Advisor at Grue Sparebank, which has sharpened how I communicate technical ideas to non-technical audiences.
